2. What it is for

Nexus CAM is a support tool for CNC turning programming and machining calculations: it reads parts from STEP files, helps build the process, simulates the tool path and generates the program for the selected control. It also includes machining and tolerance and fit calculators. It was made to make everyday work easier, give direction, and help people understand and practice the machining process and CNC programming. It is a support and learning tool, and it is not a certified CAM system nor a simulator validated for production.

The generated program and the simulation are aids and may contain errors or not reflect your machine, control, tooling, work zero and workholding. The decision to run the program on the machine, the machine setup, the cutting parameters and operator safety are entirely the responsibility of whoever machines the part.
